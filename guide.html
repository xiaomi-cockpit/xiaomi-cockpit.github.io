User guide

Using Xiaomi Cockpit

This guide walks through the app from the first install to every setting. If you only want the short version, read Getting started; the rest is there when you need it.

Getting started

Requirements

Required for the car. A supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region, and Xiaomi interconnectivity that connects the phone to your car (with the same Xiaomi account on the phone and in the car).

  • A Xiaomi phone with HyperOS and Android 12 or newer, in the Chinese region.
  • A Xiaomi car that you can connect your phone to with Xiaomi interconnectivity.
  • The real Baidu app must not be installed on the phone (see Troubleshooting).

Without interconnectivity the app can only be opened on your phone. It cannot be shown on the car screen. Everything in this guide that talks about the car needs the connection to work first.

Install the app

  1. On your phone, download Xiaomi-Cockpit.apk.
  2. Open the file. When Android asks to allow installing from your browser or file manager, allow it. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose Install anyway.
  3. Open Xiaomi Cockpit. The first start is a guided setup: your language, the permissions and your map service (Google, Yandex or None; you can change it later in the settings). For Google you also read a short screen about Google's costs, accept it and add your key.

Tip. Installing a new version on top of the old one keeps all your settings. You never need to uninstall to update, and the app can fetch updates for you (see Updating).

If the install fails with a message about conflicting signatures or an existing package, the real Baidu app (the one with the same package name) is on the phone. Uninstall it and install again.

Three more things in App info on HyperOS, so the system does not stop the app:

  2. Set Battery saver to No restrictions.
  3. Allow Display pop-up windows and Chain start.

Connect to the car

Connect your phone to the car with Xiaomi interconnectivity (the same Xiaomi account on the phone and in the car). This connection is required: without it the app only opens on the phone. Then open Xiaomi Cockpit from the app list on the car screen, or pin it to the car's home screen. The app draws itself to fit the space the car gives it, and redraws if you resize it.

The first time you launch another app from the dock, HyperOS may show a Chain start question on the phone. Choose Always allow.

The three layouts

  • Phone and tall windows: clock, live map, music and the app row stacked from top to bottom.
  • Compact car screen: apps in a column on the left, music and clock side by side on top, the map as one wide box underneath.
  • Full car screen: apps on the left, a big map in the middle, the clock above a full music player on the right.

The layout is chosen automatically from the window's shape. Nothing scrolls, and sizes are scaled so that icons and text look the same size on the phone and on each car layout.

Clock and weather

The clock card shows the time in large light digits, the date on two lines, and the weather on the right: the temperature and an icon, the condition, your city and the day's high and low.

  • Weather source: Weather comes from Google Weather (with the Google service) or from free Open-Meteo (with Yandex or no service). With Google Weather the condition words (for example Cloudy) are Google's own; with Open-Meteo they are the app's. Pick your city in Settings > Weather: Weather provider, City (type a name, tap search and choose from the list), Weather animation and Update frequency (every 15 minutes to every 3 hours, 30 minutes by default). If Google Weather does not answer, a note under the provider row says why and Open-Meteo is used for now.
  • Next events (bottom left) lists up to three upcoming events, one per line, like Today · All day · Dentist or Tue · 14:30 · Team meeting. The first part is Today, then the weekday for the next days, and a date such as Oct 12 from a week away. Long titles are cut with “…”. It needs the Calendar permission.
  • Status gauge (top right): the ring is the battery level, the Wi-Fi symbol inside shows Wi-Fi strength and the dots under it show mobile signal.

Weather effects

The dashboard shows the weather as well as telling it. The clock card has a sky with clouds that follow the condition (clear, partly cloudy, overcast, fog, rain, snow, storm). Rain falls over the whole dashboard, splashes on the top edge of the cards, and small drops form on the clock card, merge and run down the glass, bending the text behind them. Snow piles up on the top edges of the cards and the dock. Thunderstorms add lightning that flashes across the screen.

  • With the Light theme, gloomy weather darkens the whole interface and switches the live map to its night style, and at night the app switches to the dark theme by itself. With Follow phone, Dark or Auto (sunrise/sunset) your Theme setting always wins and the weather never changes it.
  • The glass edge glow (the accent light along a card's rim) stays on top of the sky and the rain on the clock card.
  • The effects are on by default. Switch everything off with Settings > Weather > Weather animation. Then the theme stays as you chose it.
  • Rain and snow only show when your city is set and the weather data is there.

Music

The music card shows what any music app is playing: the cover, the title and the artist, with previous, play / pause and next buttons.

  • When nothing is playing it says so; tap it to open your music app. Choose the app in Settings > Music > Default music app.
  • Like button: if the playing app offers a like (for example YouTube Music), a heart takes the place of the previous button. Tap to like or unlike.
  • Progress bar: the larger layouts show the elapsed time, the length and a bar you can drag to jump in the song.
  • Play when connected: optionally start playing by itself when the car connects (Settings > Look and behaviour).
  • Accent waves: soft waves in the accent colour flow behind the buttons while a song plays and fade towards the edges. The round buttons are frosted glass.
  • With the Album art accent, the colours of the app follow the cover of the current song.

Nothing shows? The music card needs Music and navigation access. Check that it says Allowed in the setup screen.

Map and navigation

The map is Google's own live map, with 3D buildings and traffic, following your car (or Yandex's map with traffic, if you choose the Yandex service). At night it switches to Google's dark map style. As you speed up it zooms out smoothly; while you drive it tilts into a driving view. Tap the expand button to make the map fill the screen.

  • Driving view: always on. The map tilts into 3D by itself once the car moves.
  • Reset button: after you drag, pinch or turn the map, a round arrow brings it back to your position. It shows only after you moved the map.
  • Round buttons on the left of the map: Charger (bolt), Search, Voice (cycles spoken guidance between silent, alerts only and guidance, Google service only), View (3D navigation view or north up) and Settings (map settings such as live traffic and satellite view). The small map card shows only Charger and Search until you expand it. While a destination is chosen or a route runs, an ✕ appears where the reset button sits.
  • Bolt button (the first round button): finds nearby EV chargers, see Nearby chargers.

Choose the service in Settings > Service > API service (the setup guide asks on the first start):

  • Google (recommended): the live map, search, route options, Google's own turn-by-turn guidance (Navigation SDK) and weather, all with your own Google key.
  • Yandex: Yandex's live map with traffic, place search, nearby chargers and a route preview with Yandex's travel time, using your own free Yandex MapKit key. There is no turn-by-turn banner, because Yandex's free licence does not include guidance. See Get a Yandex key.
  • None: only the live map with a compass and your position. There is no search, route or turn-by-turn, and the weather comes from Open-Meteo. The search button asks you to choose an API service first.

Get a Google key

Routes, search, traffic times, turn-by-turn guidance, the chargers' details and Google Weather use your own Google API key, so you never use anyone else's allowance. One key does it all. Google gives each service a free monthly allowance and charges your own Google account above it, and the app asks for your consent before it uses anything that can cost money. The key stays on your phone. The live map itself is free. Pricing and allowances are Google's and can change, so check them in Google Cloud.

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and sign in.
  2. Create a project and add a billing account (Google asks for a card, but the free monthly allowance is large).
  3. In APIs & Services > Library, turn on Map SDK for Android, Map Tiles API, Navigation SDK, Places API (New), Roads API, Routes API and Weather API.
  4. In APIs & Services > Credentials, create an API key and copy it.
  5. In the app open Settings > Service > API status and add the key (or add it in the setup guide), paste it and tap Check.

Check tries each Google service with your key. If all work, the button becomes Save. If something fails, the app tells you which one and what to fix (for example "Not turned on: press Enable in Google Cloud"). You can also choose Save anyway. The status row shows Valid (green), Needs fixing (red), Not checked or Not added (orange).

Consent. The first time you use Google, a glass screen explains the free allowances and the charge above them and asks you to accept. The acceptance is written to a consent log on your phone. In API status you can read the log, see how many guided routes you used this month and withdraw your consent: after a confirmation that removes the key from the phone and the Google services stop.

Tip. If you restrict the key to Android apps in Google Cloud, enter the app's package name and its SHA-1 fingerprint. A key without restrictions is simpler but anyone who gets it can use it, so keep it private.

Get a Yandex key

The Yandex API map service needs your own free Yandex MapKit key.

  1. Open yandex.com/maps-api/console and sign in (create a Yandex account first if you do not have one).
  2. Tap Connect on MapKit SDK. A key is created for you; copy it.
  3. In the app open Settings > Service, choose API service > Yandex and add the key in the setup guide or in API status, then tap Save. The map reloads with the new key by itself.

The Yandex key (API status) row shows Valid (green) once the map has loaded with the key, Not working yet (red) if it has not loaded, and Not checked or Not added (orange). A newly created key can take some time, sometimes 15 minutes or more, before the map loads. Once the key is valid, the How to get a key row goes away.

Set a destination

  1. Tap the magnifier (Search). A card opens at the top with Home, Work and a search field.
  2. Type a place or an address. Results appear while you type, nearest to your car first. Tap one.
  3. The destination card shows the time and distance with Start, the alternative-routes button and + to add a stop. The route is drawn on the map, coloured by traffic: blue is normal, orange is slow and red is a jam.
  4. To stop, tap the ✕ button on the map (it takes the place of the reset button while a destination is chosen or a route runs).
  5. Alternative routes: the other routes are drawn on the map in grey. Tap one to choose it; a small bubble says how many minutes faster or slower it is.
  6. Stops: tap + to add up to four stops. Tap the trip card to edit the trip: drag the stops into order or remove them.

With the Yandex API service the route is drawn with Yandex's traffic colours and travel time (a bubble shows the minutes), but the app does not guide you turn by turn.

To set Home and Work, open Settings > Map and tap the row. You can pick on the map, paste a map link (from Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, Amap, OpenStreetMap and others), type an address, or clear it. A spot picked on the map is exact.

Route overview. When you pick a destination while the car stands still, the map first zooms out to show the whole way, clear of the cards. It zooms in and follows you once you start driving or when you touch the map. Re-routing does not zoom out again. If you leave the route, guidance finds a new one at once. With Yandex, Settings > Map > Live traffic update chooses how often the route's traffic colours and travel time refresh.

The turn banner

While you drive a route, a frosted glass banner at the top of the map shows Google's own guidance: the turn arrow, the distance to the next turn in large figures, the instruction with the street name and, when another turn follows closely, a small Then line with its icon. A line shows the arrival time, the time left and the distance left. A frosted card at the bottom shows the minutes left with the ✕ (end route), alternatives and + (add stop) buttons. If you leave the route, the app finds a new one by itself. Turn-by-turn needs the Google service: Yandex and None show no banner.

Nearby chargers

Tap the bolt button, the first of the round buttons on the live map. Green charger pins appear around you and the nearest charger is chosen at once. A card shows its name, the distance and drive time, every connector type known for the station (like CCS2 · GB/T) and where it is, smallest area first, for example Nirouth · Chbar Ampov · Phnom Penh. A part that is not known is simply left out.

  • The 15 closest chargers are found, wherever they are (there is no distance limit, so in a remote area they can be in the next district or province), with the quickest to reach first.
  • A bar at the bottom steps through the other stations with arrows. The map zooms to show you, the charger and the blue route between, and the other chargers as pins.
  • Go starts the route to the chosen charger. The ✕ where the reset button sits cancels your choice.
  • With your Google key the list comes from Google Places with the connector types; without a key, or when Google finds nothing, it comes from OpenStreetMap, which often has less detail (a station may then show no connector types).
  • While you drive a route, the bolt button hides the route for a moment and the card says Add: the charger becomes a stop before your destination and the route is recalculated. If you cancel, the old route comes back.

Phone notifications

With Notification access allowed, a frosted glass card slides in at the top of the dashboard when your phone gets a notification, so you can see it on the car screen.

  • Swipe the card up, or tap outside it, to dismiss it. Touches do not go through the card to the map below.
  • Turn it off in Settings > Look and behaviour > Show phone notifications on the dashboard.
  • The text is read on the phone only and is never sent anywhere.

The app dock

The dock holds your favourite apps. Tapping one opens it on the same screen the dashboard is on, so on the car screen the app opens on the car.

  • All apps: the last button opens a sheet with every app on the phone.
  • Edit the dock: press and hold the dock. The icons wobble slowly. Drag to reorder, tap the round badge at the top right of an icon to remove it, and tap the + button at the end to add apps (the list has search and app icons). Tap outside the dock, or open All apps, to finish.

Floating return button

When you open another app on the car screen, a small round glass button stays on top. Tap it once to come back to the cockpit; it blinks while it works and can take a second or two.

  • Move it by dragging it anywhere on the screen. It stays where you drop it.
  • It fades when you are not using it.
  • Turn it on or off in Settings > Look and behaviour > Floating home button. It is off by default, and it is never shown on the phone's own screen.
  • A silent notification keeps the button alive. To hide it, use Hide the floating button notification in the settings; the button keeps working.

Tip. The system back swipe on the car screen also returns you, even without the button.

Looks and themes

  • Theme: Follow phone, Dark, Light or Auto (sunrise/sunset), which is dark between sunset and sunrise at your weather city. The dashboard switches by itself, with a smooth fade, when the phone changes mode, when the sun sets or rises, or when you change the setting. Your choice always wins: only Light lets the weather effects turn the screen dark at night.
  • Background: the colour styles Cockpit, Dusk, Forest, Graphite and Slate, or My photo from your gallery. A press and hold on an empty spot of the dashboard opens the picker too.
  • Animated background: soft moving light and large colour bubbles behind the glass (not used with a photo).
  • Accent colour: Mint, Sky, Violet, Pink, Orange, Yellow, Red, Lime, a custom colour, or Album art to follow the song.
  • Weather animation (in Settings > Weather): the sky, rain, snow and lightning, and (with the Light theme only) the gloomy look and the automatic dark theme at night.
  • Power saver: turns off the animated background, the weather effects and the 3D buildings and updates the map less often. It also switches on by itself while the phone's battery saver is on or the phone is hot.
  • The glass shadow under the panels and the glass edge refraction (Android 13 and newer: the rim of each panel, and of the live map, picks up the accent light of the bubbles behind it) are always on.
  • Play music when connected to car, Show phone notifications on the dashboard, Floating home button.
  • Language: English, Khmer, Russian or Polish.

Troubleshooting

The app does not appear in the car's app list

First check the requirements: a supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region and a working Xiaomi interconnectivity connection to the car. Make sure the phone and the car use the same Xiaomi account and that interconnectivity is connected. The car only lists apps it recognises, and Xiaomi Cockpit uses a package name for that reason; if the real Baidu app is installed on the phone, remove it and install Xiaomi Cockpit again.

The floating button does not bring the dashboard back

Tap once and wait two seconds (taps while it works are ignored). Check that Display over other apps is allowed, Autostart is on, and Battery saver is set to No restrictions. As a fallback use the system back swipe.

A permission switch is greyed out

Open App info, tap the three dots at the top and choose Unlock permissions, then try the switch again.

Music or the track title does not show

Check Music and navigation access. Start a song in your music app and give it a few seconds. Some apps do not offer a like button; then the previous button stays.

The map says "Allow location to show the map"

Allow Location in the setup screen and, for the car screen, check that the phone has a GPS fix (go outside or near a window).

Home, Work or search asks for a Google key

These need your own key. Follow Get a Google key. If Check reports a problem, fix the item it names (usually turning on one of the APIs or adding a billing account).

The Yandex map is black or says it is not loading

A newly created Yandex key can take 15 minutes or more before it works; wait a little and look at Yandex key in Settings > Service > API status. Check the internet connection and that the key was copied completely. If it stays red, create the key again in the Yandex console (Get a Yandex key).

"Finding the route…" never finishes

Check the internet connection on the phone and the key status in Settings > Service > API status. Press Check again to see which service fails.

The weather is empty

Choose your city in Settings > Weather, and check the phone has internet. If you chose Google Weather and it does not answer, a note under Weather provider says why (often the Weather API is not turned on for your key); the app then uses Open-Meteo.

Nearby chargers shows nothing, or too few

Check the internet connection and that Location is allowed. Without a Google key the list comes from OpenStreetMap, which may know fewer stations in your area. With a key, make sure Places API (New) is turned on (Check in Settings > Service > API status tells you). Use Report in the settings while the card is open if it still shows nothing.

The update does not install

The first time, Android asks you to allow this app to install apps: tap Open settings, switch on Allow from this source, come back and tap Update now again. If the download fails, check the internet connection. You can always download the newest APK from the Updating section and install it over the old one.

Something else is wrong, or it still does not work

Use Report a problem in the settings. Add a screenshot and keep the log on, then it is much easier to find the cause.

The layout looks too big or too small on the car

The app scales each car layout so it matches the phone. If it still looks off on your car, send a photo of the screen and the car model so it can be tuned.

Report a problem

If something does not work, you can tell the developer from inside the app. You do not need a GitHub account.

  1. Open the settings (swipe down on the dashboard, tap the gear) and find Support.
  2. Tap Report next to Report a problem. For an idea, tap Request next to Request a feature instead; it works the same way.
  3. Describe what went wrong and what you expected. Add up to three screenshots from your gallery with Add. Keep Include the app log on: it helps a lot. If the app crashed, the crash is included automatically.
  4. Tap Review. The next screen shows exactly what will be public. Scroll it to read everything.
  5. Tap Send. You get a report number and an Open on GitHub button.

What is public and what is private

  • Public: your description, the app version, the phone model and Android version, which layout was used, the state of the map and route services, and the last lines of the app log. Everyone can read this on the public list of reports.
  • Private: your screenshots and the full log. Only the developer can open them.
  • Removed before sending: your Google key, the Home and Work addresses, positions and e-mail addresses. Screenshots are not edited, so avoid showing personal details in them.
  • One report per minute can be sent. If sending fails (no internet, for example), the app offers to share the text through your phone's share sheet instead.

Following your report: open the link from the Open on GitHub button, or look for the number on the reports page. Answers and the fix are written there, and the fixed version is listed in the release notes.

Updating

The app can update itself. Open Settings > Updates and tap Check for updates: it looks at the project's published releases, shows what is new and, if you agree, downloads the new version and asks Android to install it over the old one (the first time Android may ask you to allow installing from this app). Check for updates automatically (on by default) does the same check by itself on the phone, at most every 12 hours, and asks before it installs anything. Your settings, key, Home and Work are kept.

You can also download the newest Xiaomi-Cockpit.apk and install it over the old one. Your settings, key, Home and Work are kept. The releases page lists what changed in each version.
