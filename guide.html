User guide

Using Xiaomi Cockpit

This guide walks through the app from the first install to every setting. If you only want the short version, read Getting started; the rest is there when you need it.

Getting started

Requirements

Required for the car. A supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region, and Xiaomi interconnectivity that connects the phone to your car (with the same Xiaomi account on the phone and in the car).

  • A Xiaomi phone with HyperOS and Android 12 or newer, in the Chinese region.
  • A Xiaomi car that you can connect your phone to with Xiaomi interconnectivity.
  • The real Baidu app must not be installed on the phone (see Troubleshooting).

Without interconnectivity the app can only be opened on your phone. It cannot be shown on the car screen. Everything in this guide that talks about the car needs the connection to work first.

Install the app

  1. On your phone, download Xiaomi-Cockpit.apk.
  2. Open the file. When Android asks to allow installing from your browser or file manager, allow it. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose Install anyway.
  3. Open Xiaomi Cockpit. Choose your language, then go through the setup screen.

Tip. Installing a new version on top of the old one keeps all your settings. You never need to uninstall to update.

If the install fails with a message about conflicting signatures or an existing package, the real Baidu app (the one with the same package name) is on the phone. Uninstall it and install again.

Allow what it needs

The setup screen (and Settings > Setup) lists what the app asks for. Each row shows Allowed or Tap to allow.

PermissionWhat it is used for

NotificationsA quiet notification that keeps the floating button alive.
Location (speed and map)Your position for the live map, speed and navigation.

If a switch is greyed out: open App info (the setup screen has a button for it), tap the three-dot menu at the top and choose Unlock permissions. HyperOS blocks some permissions for apps installed from a file until you do this.

Three more things in App info on HyperOS, so the system does not stop the app:

  1. Turn on Autostart.
  2. Set Battery saver to No restrictions.
  3. Allow Display pop-up windows and Chain start.

Connect to the car

Connect your phone to the car with Xiaomi interconnectivity (the same Xiaomi account on the phone and in the car). This connection is required: without it the app only opens on the phone. Then open Xiaomi Cockpit from the app list on the car screen, or pin it to the car's home screen. The app draws itself to fit the space the car gives it, and redraws if you resize it.

The first time you launch another app from the dock, HyperOS may show a Chain start question on the phone. Choose Always allow.

The three layouts

  • Phone and tall windows: clock, live map, music and the app row stacked from top to bottom.
  • Compact car screen: apps in a column on the left, music and clock side by side on top, the map as one wide box underneath.
  • Full car screen: apps on the left, a big map in the middle, the clock above a full music player on the right.

The layout is chosen automatically from the window's shape. Nothing scrolls, and sizes are scaled so that icons and text look the same size on the phone and on each car layout.

Clock and weather

The clock card shows the time in large light digits, the date on two lines, and the weather on the right: the temperature and an icon, the condition, your city and the day's high and low.

  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo and refreshes about every 30 minutes. Pick your city in Settings > Weather: type a name, tap search and choose from the list.
  • Next event (bottom left) shows the date and time, how long it lasts, and its title. It needs the Calendar permission. When there is not much room, the length is left out.
  • Status gauge (top right): the ring is the battery level, the Wi-Fi symbol inside shows Wi-Fi strength and the dots under it show mobile signal.

Music

The music card shows what any music app is playing: the cover, the title and the artist, with previous, play / pause and next buttons.

  • When nothing is playing it says so; tap it to open your music app. Choose the app in Settings > Dashboard apps > Default music app.
  • Like button: if the playing app offers a like (for example YouTube Music), a heart takes the place of the previous button. Tap to like or unlike. You can turn this off in the settings.
  • Progress bar: the larger layouts show the elapsed time, the length and a bar you can drag to jump in the song.
  • Play when connected: optionally start playing by itself when the car connects (Settings > Look and behaviour).
  • Music bars: five small bars next to the title (between the title and the buttons in the phone layout) move while a song plays and rest when it is paused. By default they move on their own. Switch on Settings > Look and behaviour > Visualizer follows the music and they follow the real sound: the bass and the drums on the left, the treble on the right. This needs the audio permission, which the phone asks for when you switch it on. The app only measures how loud the music is; nothing is recorded or saved. Some phones or music apps give no sound data; then the bars keep their own movement (Settings > Diagnostics > Visualizer shows what happens).
  • With the Album art accent, the colours of the app follow the cover of the current song.

Nothing shows? The music card needs Music and navigation access. Check that it says Allowed in the setup screen.

Map and navigation

The map is Google's own live map, with 3D buildings and traffic, following your car. As you speed up it zooms out smoothly; while you drive it tilts into a driving view.

  • Car button: switches between the tilted driving view and a flat map.
  • Compass button: after you drag or pinch the map, this brings it back to your position.
  • Home and Work buttons start a route to the places you saved. Set destination (or the magnifier in landscape) lets you search anywhere.

You can choose how navigation works in Settings > Maps > Map service:

  • Google API (recommended): routes, traffic and turn-by-turn all run inside the dashboard, using your own Google key.
  • Custom Maps App: the dashboard shows the live map and reads turn-by-turn, arrival time and time left from the maps app you choose (it must show a navigation notification). Home and Work open that app, and tapping the map opens it too. No routing happens in the dashboard in this mode.

Get a Google key

Routes, destination search and traffic times use your own Google API key, so you never use anyone else's allowance. Google gives a free monthly allowance, and the key stays on your phone. The live map works without it.

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and sign in.
  2. Create a project and add a billing account (Google asks for a card, but the free monthly allowance is large).
  3. In APIs & Services > Library, turn on Routes API, Places API (New) and Map Tiles API.
  4. In APIs & Services > Credentials, create an API key and copy it.
  5. In the app open Settings > Maps > Google key > Add key, paste the key and tap Check.

Check tries the three services with your key. If all work, the button becomes Save. If something fails, the app tells you which one and what to fix (for example "Not turned on: press Enable in Google Cloud"). You can also choose Save anyway. The status row shows Valid, Needs fixing or Not added.

Tip. If you restrict the key to Android apps in Google Cloud, enter the app's package name and its SHA-1 fingerprint. A key without restrictions is simpler but anyone who gets it can use it, so keep it private.

Set a destination

  1. Tap Home or Work, or tap Set destination / the magnifier.
  2. Type a place or an address. Results appear while you type, nearest to your car first. Tap one.
  3. The route is drawn on the map, coloured by traffic: blue is normal, orange is slow and red is a jam.
  4. To stop, tap End route (or the ✕ button in landscape).

To set Home and Work, open Settings > Maps and tap the row. You can pick on the map, paste a map link (from Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, Amap, OpenStreetMap and others), type an address, or clear it. A spot picked on the map is exact.

The turn banner

While you drive a route, a glass banner at the top of the map shows the distance to the next turn in large figures, the instruction with the street name in bold (roundabouts and U-turns have their own symbols), and a small line with the arrival time, the time left and the distance left. A round bubble in the corner shows the minutes left. If you leave the route, the app finds a new one by itself.

The app dock

The dock holds your favourite apps. Tapping one opens it on the same screen the dashboard is on, so on the car screen the app opens on the car.

  • All apps: the last button opens a sheet with every app on the phone.
  • Change the dock: press and hold the all-apps button (or tap the plus button when the dock is empty), then tick the apps you want. The picker has search and shows the app icons.
  • App names: you can show names under the icons in Settings > Look and behaviour.

Floating return button

When you open another app on the car screen, a small round glass button stays on top. Tap it once to come back to the cockpit; it blinks while it works and can take a second or two.

  • Move it by dragging it anywhere on the screen. It stays where you drop it.
  • It fades when you are not using it.
  • Turn it on or off in Settings > Look and behaviour > Floating home button. By default it is not shown on the phone's own screen; switch on Floating button on phone screen if you want it there too.
  • A silent notification keeps the button alive. To hide it, use Hide the floating button notification in the settings; the button keeps working.

Tip. The system back swipe on the car screen also returns you, even without the button.

Settings, one by one

Open the settings by swiping down on the dashboard and tapping the gear. Use the back button to return; there is nothing to save.

Looks and themes

  • Theme: Follow phone, Dark or Light.
  • Background: the colour styles Cockpit, Dusk, Forest, Graphite and Slate, or My photo from your gallery. A press and hold on an empty spot of the dashboard opens the picker too.
  • Animated background: soft moving light and large colour bubbles behind the glass (not used with a photo).
  • Accent colour: Mint, Sky, Violet, Pink, Orange, Yellow, Red, Lime, a custom colour, or Album art to follow the song.
  • Glass edge refraction (Android 13 and newer): the rim of each panel picks up the light and colours behind it.
  • Glass shadow: a soft shadow under the bottom edge of the panels.
  • Like button in music controls, Visualizer follows the music, Play music when connected to car, Show app names under shortcuts.
  • Language: English, Khmer, Russian or Polish.

Troubleshooting

The app does not appear in the car's app list

First check the requirements: a supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region and a working Xiaomi interconnectivity connection to the car. Make sure the phone and the car use the same Xiaomi account and that interconnectivity is connected. The car only lists apps it recognises, and Xiaomi Cockpit uses a package name for that reason; if the real Baidu app is installed on the phone, remove it and install Xiaomi Cockpit again.

The floating button does not bring the dashboard back

Tap once and wait two seconds (taps while it works are ignored). Check that Display over other apps is allowed, Autostart is on, and Battery saver is set to No restrictions. As a fallback use the system back swipe.

A permission switch is greyed out

Open App info, tap the three dots at the top and choose Unlock permissions, then try the switch again.

Music or the track title does not show

Check Music and navigation access. Start a song in your music app and give it a few seconds. Some apps do not offer a like button; then the previous button stays.

The map says "Allow location to show the map"

Allow Location in the setup screen and, for the car screen, check that the phone has a GPS fix (go outside or near a window).

Home, Work or "Set destination" asks for a Google key

These need your own key. Follow Get a Google key. If Check reports a problem, fix the item it names (usually turning on one of the three APIs or adding a billing account).

"Finding the route…" never finishes

Check the internet connection on the phone and the key status in Settings > Maps. Press Check again to see which service fails.

The weather is empty

Choose your city in Settings > Weather, and check the phone has internet.

Something else is wrong, or it still does not work

Use Report a problem in the settings. Add a screenshot and keep the log on, then it is much easier to find the cause.

The layout looks too big or too small on the car

The app scales each car layout so it matches the phone. If it still looks off on your car, send a photo of the screen and the car model so it can be tuned.

Report a problem

If something does not work, you can tell the developer from inside the app. You do not need a GitHub account.

  1. Open the settings (swipe down on the dashboard, tap the gear) and find Support.
  2. Tap Write next to Report a problem.
  3. Describe what went wrong and what you expected. Add up to three screenshots from your gallery with Add. Keep Include the app log on: it helps a lot. If the app crashed, the crash is included automatically.
  4. Tap Review. The next screen shows exactly what will be public. Scroll it to read everything.
  5. Tap Send. You get a report number and an Open on GitHub button.

What is public and what is private

  • Public: your description, the app version, the phone model and Android version, which layout was used, the state of the map and route services, and the last lines of the app log. Everyone can read this on the public list of reports.
  • Private: your screenshots and the full log. Only the developer can open them.
  • Removed before sending: your Google key, the Home and Work addresses, positions and e-mail addresses. Screenshots are not edited, so avoid showing personal details in them.
  • One report per minute can be sent. If sending fails (no internet, for example), the app offers to share the text through your phone's share sheet instead.

Following your report: open the link from the Open on GitHub button, or look for the number on the reports page. Answers and the fix are written there, and the fixed version is listed in the release notes.

Updating

Download the newest Xiaomi-Cockpit.apk and install it over the old one. Your settings, key, Home and Work are kept. The releases page lists what changed in each version.
