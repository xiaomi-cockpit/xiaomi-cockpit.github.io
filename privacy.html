Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026. This policy covers the Android app Xiaomi Cockpit and this website. Xiaomi Cockpit is a free, independent community project. The short version: the app has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and the people who make it run no server that collects your data. Almost everything stays on your phone. The few things that leave it go to the services you choose to use, and this page lists each one.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, your dock apps, your Home and Work places, your city, your background photo and your language.
  • Your Google key and your Yandex key. They are kept on the phone and are only sent to Google or Yandex when the app asks those services for something.
  • The music that is playing (title, artist, cover) and the text of the notifications your phone shows. The app reads them from the system to show them on the dashboard (the music card, and the notification card if you leave it on). They are not sent anywhere.
  • The next events of your calendar, read from the phone's calendar to show on the clock card. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Your consent to Google's costs and the consent log (date, text version, app version and a short fingerprint of your key). It never leaves the phone.

What is sent, and to whom

The app asks for these permissions: location, notification access, notifications, calendar and display over other apps. Location is used on the phone for the map and the speed. Only the services below receive anything, and only when the matching feature is used.

Your own Google account and the costs

The app uses the key of your own Google Cloud project, so Google sees the use under your account, not under ours. Before any Google service that can cost money is used, a screen explains the free monthly allowances and the charge above them, and asks you to accept. The acceptance (or its withdrawal) is written to the consent log on your phone. You can read the log, and withdraw your consent, in Settings > Service > API status. When you withdraw, and confirm, your Google key is removed from the phone and the Google services and the navigation stop at once.

Reports you send

A report or idea sent from the app goes to the project's public issue list on GitHub, so everyone can see and follow it. The app removes your Google key, addresses and positions from it first. Screenshots and the full log stay private to the project. Please do not write personal details in a report.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in, shows no advertising and has no analytics or tracking tool.
  • It does not sell or share your data, and it does not send anything to the makers of the app, other than a report you send yourself.
  • It does not read your messages, contacts, photos (except a background photo you choose) or other apps. Notification text is only shown on the dashboard, on the phone, and is never stored or sent.

Your choices

  • Withdraw your consent in Settings > Service > API status. Your Google key is removed and the Google services stop.
  • Remove your Google key or your Yandex key in Settings > Service > API status.
  • Refuse any permission in Android's settings. The matching feature then stops working.
  • Delete everything the app stored: clear the app's data or uninstall it in Android's settings.

Children

The app is made for drivers and is not aimed at children.

This website

The website is a static page on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics and sets no cookies. It remembers your theme and language in your browser (local storage), and nothing else. The home page asks GitHub's public API for the number of the latest version, to show it on the download button. GitHub, which hosts the site, sees your IP address when you visit and handles it under its own privacy statement.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and the consent screen asks you again if the costs text changed. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/xiaomi-cockpit/xiaomi-cockpit.github.io/issues.
