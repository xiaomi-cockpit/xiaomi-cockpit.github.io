Features

Everything you glance at while driving, in one place

Big, calm and easy to hit on a car touch screen. Dark and light themes, a moving cockpit background and glass panels that pick up the accent colour.

Clock and weather

A big light clock, the date, live weather with the day's high and low, and your next calendar event with its time and length. A small gauge shows battery, Wi-Fi and mobile signal.

Music

Shows what is playing in any music app: cover art, title, progress you can drag, previous, play and next, and a like button when the app supports it. Small music bars next to the title can follow the beat of the song, and the accent colour can follow the album cover.

Live map

Google's map with 3D buildings and live traffic follows your car. It zooms out as you speed up, tilts when you drive, and you can pinch, drag and snap back to your position.

Navigation

Pick Home, Work, search a place or choose a spot on the map. See the route coloured by traffic, a turn-by-turn banner with the street name, time left and arrival time, with automatic re-routing.

App dock

Your favourite apps one tap away, opened on the car screen. Tap the all-apps button to see every app, search them, and long-press to change the shortcuts.

Floating return button

When you open another app on the car screen, a small glass button stays on top. Tap it to come straight back to the cockpit. Drag it anywhere you like.

Liquid glass design

Frosted panels with a lit top edge, a soft shadow below and an accent glow along the rim. Choose a background, an accent colour or your own photo, in a dark or a light theme.

Fits every screen

One layout for the phone and two for the car: a compact landscape and a roomy full screen. Sizes are scaled so buttons and text look the same on each.

Your language

English, Khmer, Russian and Polish, including dates, units, the map and the turn-by-turn directions. More can be added.

Download and install

Up and running in a few minutes

Xiaomi Cockpit is not on an app store, so you install the APK file yourself. It takes about five minutes the first time.

Before you install: what is required

Xiaomi Cockpit is made for the Xiaomi phone-to-car connection. To use it in the car you need:

  • A supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region (China-region system software).
  • Xiaomi interconnectivity that connects to your car, with the same Xiaomi account on the phone and in the car.

Without interconnectivity the app can only be opened on your phone. It will not show on the car screen.

What you need

  • A supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region, with HyperOS and Android 12 or newer.
  • A Xiaomi car, and Xiaomi interconnectivity that connects the phone to the car, with the same Xiaomi account on both.
  • The real Baidu app must not be installed on the phone (see the FAQ).

Optional: a Google key

The live map works right away. For routes, destination search and traffic times you add your own free Google API key. The guide shows every click.

How to get a key

  1. Download the APK on your phone

    Tap the download button above in your phone's browser. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, choose to keep it. It is an app file that does not come from a store.

  2. Install it

    Open the downloaded file. Android asks you to allow installing apps from this source: allow it, then tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway. Installing a newer version over an old one keeps your settings.

  3. Open the app and choose your language

    The first start asks for a language and then opens the setup screen.

  4. Allow what the app needs

    On the setup screen tap each row and allow it:

    • Display over other apps: for the floating return button.
    • Music and navigation access: so the app can show what is playing.
    • Notifications, Location (speed and map) and Calendar (next event).

    If a switch is greyed out, open App info, tap the three-dot menu and choose Unlock permissions, then try again. On HyperOS also turn on Autostart, set Battery saver to No restrictions, and allow Display pop-up windows and Chain start.

  5. Connect to the car

    Connect your phone to the car with Xiaomi interconnectivity (required for the car screen) and open Xiaomi Cockpit from the app list on the car screen, or pin it to the car's home screen. The dashboard fits the window it is shown in.

  6. Set your city and your places

    Open the settings (swipe down on the dashboard and tap the gear). Choose your city for the weather, set Home and Work, and pick your dock apps. The user guide explains every setting.

Questions

Good to know

Does it work without Xiaomi interconnectivity?

Only on the phone. Without interconnectivity the app can be opened on your phone, but it cannot be shown on the car screen. To use it in the car you need a supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region that can connect to the car with Xiaomi interconnectivity.

Is it free? Is there an account or advertising?

It is free, community built, and has no account and no advertising.

Why does the app use the package name com.baidu.searchbox?

The car only lists apps it knows. Xiaomi Cockpit uses a package name the car already accepts so it can appear on the car screen. The side effect is that it cannot be on the real Baidu app's phone at the same time: if the real Baidu app is installed, Android refuses the install with a "conflicting signatures" message. Uninstall the Baidu app first.

Do I need a Google API key?

Not to see the map. The live Google map is built in. The key is needed for routes, destination search and traffic times, so Home, Work and "Set destination" work. Google gives a free monthly allowance, and the key stays on your phone. See the guide.

The floating button does not bring the cockpit back

Tap it once and wait about two seconds. Make sure Display over other apps is allowed and Autostart is on for the app. More tips are in the troubleshooting section.

Something does not work. How do I report it?

Open Settings > Support > Report a problem in the app, describe the problem and add screenshots. It opens a public report you can follow, and the screenshots and full log stay private. See Support.

Is it made by Xiaomi?

No. It is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Xiaomi, Baidu or Google. Names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Support

Found a problem? Report it from inside the app

No account and no typing of logs by hand: describe what went wrong, add screenshots and the app sends everything the developer needs. You can follow your report on GitHub afterwards.

How to report

  • Swipe down on the dashboard and tap the gear to open the settings.
  • Under Support, tap Report a problem.
  • Describe what happened, add screenshots and keep Include the app log on.
  • Check the preview and tap Send. You get a report number and a link to follow it.

What is shared

  • Public: your description, the app and phone details and the last lines of the log.
  • Private: the screenshots and the full log. Only the developer can open them.
  • Removed first: your Google key, Home and Work addresses, positions and e-mail addresses.
  • You see the public text before sending, and nothing leaves the phone until you tap Send.
